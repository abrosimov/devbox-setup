# [ADR-091] - Nightly export of billing data to the warehouse (invented fixture)

Source: https://wiki.example.com/pages/9191/ADR-091 , page version 3. Status: Proposed. Nothing in
this ADR is built yet.

## Context

Finance reads billing data from the production database during the day, which slows the billing
service at month end.

## Decision

Copy billing data to the warehouse every night in an **export window** (01:00–04:00 UTC). A
**loader** reads the billing tables in batches and writes them to **landing tables** in the
warehouse. Outside the export window the landing tables are read-only.

The loader uses the vendor's bulk-load client.

## Consequences

Finance reads the warehouse only. Reports show data up to the previous night.

## Glossary

- **Export window** - the nightly period in which billing data is copied.
- **Loader** - the job that copies billing data in batches.
- **Landing tables** - the warehouse tables the loader writes to.
